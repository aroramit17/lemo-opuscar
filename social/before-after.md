# Before / After · the transformation reveal

> **Job:** show results, drive follows and saves. **Voice:** optional. **Loops:** yes. **Length:** 12–30 s.

## 1. Essence
One subject, two states, one decisive transition. Same framing, same position; only the change differs. The transition is the product. Works for design, code, rooms, spaces, workflows, processes and illustrations drawn in code.

## 2. Look
- Locked, identical framing for both states so the eye reads the difference without effort.
- "Before" slightly desaturated and flat; "after" clean and saturated.
- Minimal text: "Before", "After", and a 2–4 word result label ("-6 hours a week").

## 3. Timing grammar
- 0–2 s: the Before, with its flaw obvious.
- 2–7 s: the process compressed: time-lapse, strikes, builds, a wipe line sweeping the frame.
- 7–11 s: the After, held 1.5 s.
- Loop: the After cross-dissolves back into the Before on the same wipe so the video repeats endlessly.

## 4. Sound
Gritty low tone for Before; a riser during the sweep; a clean chime and brighter bed for After.

## 5. Native moves
- **Wipe-line sweep.** A vertical or diagonal seam drags across the frame. *Fits:* design refreshes; code clean-ups; rooms.
- **Time-lapse build.** Pieces assemble in fast, rhythmic steps. *Fits:* dashboards; layouts; recipes.
- **Flip card.** The frame flips like a card to its other side. *Fits:* myth/fact; old/new; mistakes/fixes.

## 6. Pitfalls
Different framing between states; faked results presented as real; text that explains what the picture already shows.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *wipe*, *flip*, *progressive build*. Endings: hold on After; reverse to Before; a stat label that drops in.
