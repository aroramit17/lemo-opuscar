# Satisfying Loop · hypnotic, seamless, sound-led

> **Job:** attention, brand mood, background content, audio-first posts. **Voice:** none. **Loops:** **required**. **Length:** 8–20 s.

## 1. Essence
Procedural motion that is pleasing to watch and designed to repeat without a seam: gears and marbles, sorting bars, flowing gradients, growing patterns, paint or sand layers. The sound is as important as the picture. Not a screensaver: it needs a rhythm, a small build and a "click" payoff each cycle.

## 2. Look
- Strong geometric forms, high colour contrast, a restrained 3–5 colour palette per piece.
- Deterministic, time-looped functions: every parameter is a function of `t mod DUR`, with `render(0)` equal to `render(DUR)`.
- Optional brand line at the end of the cycle as a quiet watermark-free text; no logo sting.

## 3. Timing grammar
- Cycle 4–10 s; deliver 2–3 cycles if the platform rewards watch time.
- Build (60%) → peak (20%) → release/reset (20%), on a tempo of 90–120 BPM with every event on the grid.
- The reset is the loop seam; hide it behind the peak's flash or a symmetric motion.

## 4. Sound
Sampled instruments plus procedural foley that match each event (ticks, marimba, glass, water, clicks); every visual event has a sound with a matching pitch; the last note resolves into the first for a seamless loop.

## 5. Native moves
- **Domino cascade.** Objects trigger each other left-to-right in time. *Fits:* brand moods; process visualisations; intros.
- **Sort and settle.** Chaos orders itself to a perfect pattern. *Fits:* organisation tools; data products; wellness.
- **Pour and fill.** Colour fills a shape and drains into the start. *Fits:* progress; beverages; finance metaphors.

## 6. Pitfalls
A visible seam; asynchronous sound; too many colours; noise that is "busy" rather than rhythmic; durations that don't divide the audio loop evenly.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *cascade*, *sort*, *pour*. Endings: none — it loops. Verify the seam by rendering frame 0 and frame DUR and diffing them.
