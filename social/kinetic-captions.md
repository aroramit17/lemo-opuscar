# Kinetic Captions · voice-led talking points

> **Job:** teach, argue, tell. A voice carries the idea and the words themselves become the picture. For founders, coaches, creators and anyone who doesn't want to be on camera.
> **Loops:** optional. **Voice:** required. **Length:** 20–45 s.

## 1. Essence
Big words, spoken-word timing. Each phrase lands as 1–4 words that pop, scale, or slide on the exact syllable. One highlighted word per phrase. Not a lyric video (no melody), not a slide deck (no bullet lists).

## 2. Look
- Dark or saturated flat ground with one subtle moving layer (slow gradient, grain, drifting shapes) so the frame never feels frozen.
- Type is the hero: a heavy grotesque (e.g. Inter Black, Archivo Black; OFL). 80–120 px, centred in the lower-middle band, inside the safe zone.
- Two colours maximum for text: base (high contrast) and one accent for the highlighted word.
- Optional supporting visuals: icons, simple diagrams or one object per beat, drawn in code, entering with the word they illustrate.

## 3. Timing grammar
- A word group enters on the first syllable and leaves on the next group's first syllable (no gaps, no overlap longer than 80 ms).
- Pop: scale 0.85→1.04→1 in ~160 ms with a short tick sound. Emphasis words get a bigger pop and a sound hit.
- Every 5–8 words, change something: ground colour, camera push, accent colour, background shape.
- Hook line is on screen from frame 1, voice starts within 0.2 s.

## 4. Sound
Close dry voice, quiet pulse or lo-fi bed 18 dB under, tick per group, whoosh per ground change, a low hit on the key claim.

## 5. Native moves
- **Keyword burst.** The key word scales to full frame for 6 frames, then settles. *Fits:* a number; the thing to avoid; the answer to a question.
- **Strike-through correction.** A wrong phrase appears, is struck, and the right one replaces it. *Fits:* myth vs. fact; "don't say X, say Y"; before/after wording.
- **Counter ticker.** A figure counts up with the voice. *Fits:* savings; followers; hours; price.

## 6. Pitfalls
Captions that trail the voice (build from `words.json`, not guesses); more than 4 words on screen; five colours; text on the bottom 400 px; motion that never rests, so nothing feels emphasised.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *myth → truth → proof*; *3 rules countdown*; *story → lesson*. Hooks: a number, a contradiction, a question. Endings: a one-line takeaway, a keyword CTA, a looping repeat of the hook.
