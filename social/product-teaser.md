# Product Teaser · problem → demo → CTA in 20 seconds

> **Job:** sell. **Voice:** optional (VO or text-only). **Loops:** no. **Length:** 15–30 s.

## 1. Essence
Show the problem as a moment the viewer recognises, then the product solving it in one continuous demonstration, then a single offer. Clean, tactile, confident. Not a corporate promo, not an unboxing, not a feature tour.

## 2. Look
- Product (or a stand-in drawn in code if no asset is provided) large and centred in the safe area; soft studio light, one accent colour from the brand.
- Problem scene: desaturated, cluttered. Solution scene: saturated, clean. The colour change is the story.
- Callout labels with thin leader lines, ≤ 3 words each, used sparingly.
- Use the user's logo and product images only if they supplied them and have the rights.

## 3. Timing grammar
- 0–3 s: the problem, stated as pain in ≤ 6 words, shown not told.
- 3–6 s: the turn: "what if..." and the product enters with a sound hit.
- 6–18 s: **one** demonstration, one benefit at a time, max three, each ≤ 4 s, each with a visible before/after.
- 18–22 s: proof or number (user-supplied), then CTA with price or offer if there is one.

## 4. Sound
Tight kick-and-click groove, product foley (clicks, snaps, taps), a clean riser into the product entrance, short silence before the CTA.

## 5. Native moves
- **Clutter sweep.** A wipe clears a messy scene to reveal the clean one. *Fits:* storage; cleaning; software; finance.
- **Exploded callout.** Parts separate and label themselves, then reassemble. *Fits:* gadgets; apps with layers; kits.
- **Price drop.** The number lands with a stamp and the ground flashes accent. *Fits:* offers; launches; limited drops.

## 6. Pitfalls
Opening on the logo; listing every feature; unsupported claims ("best", "#1") with no source; fake reviews or testimonials; invented statistics.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *problem-solution*, *3 benefits in 3 colours*, *day-in-the-life before/after*. Endings: offer, waitlist keyword, "link in bio".
