# Listicle Countdown · "N things" with a numbered spine

> **Job:** teach, entertain, save-worthy tips. **Voice:** optional. **Loops:** no. **Length:** 20–45 s.

## 1. Essence
A big number is the structural spine. Each item gets exactly one number card, one headline of ≤ 5 words and one visual proof. The count itself is the retention device.

## 2. Look
- One flat colour per item, rotating through a 4–5 colour set; numerals at 400–600 px as the dominant shape.
- A consistent card grammar: numeral → headline → 1 supporting image or diagram drawn in code → out.
- Progress rail at the top-safe edge showing "2/5".

## 3. Timing grammar
- Hook (0–3 s): the promise and the count ("5 mistakes that kill your reach"), with the strongest item teased.
- Each item 4–7 s; item 1 is the quickest, the best item is **last** (countdown) or **first** (ranking), never in the middle.
- Items enter on the beat; the numeral wipes out on a whoosh that doubles as the next item's whoosh-in.
- Last item holds 1 s longer, then a 1.5 s CTA.

## 4. Sound
Metronomic pulse (one hit per item), pitched tick per numeral, rising pitch across items, a payoff chord on the last.

## 5. Native moves
- **Numeral wipe.** The number scales through the screen, becoming the next item's ground colour. *Fits:* steps; mistakes; tools; stops on a trip.
- **Shuffle reveal.** Items shuffle and the winner lifts out. *Fits:* "which is best"; rankings; picks.
- **Checklist tick.** An empty tick-box fills as the item lands. *Fits:* routines; packing lists; audits.

## 6. Pitfalls
More than 7 items; items of different weight (all must be the same size of idea); explaining instead of showing; best item hidden in the middle.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *countdown*, *ranking best-first*, *good / bad pairs*. Endings: "save this for later", a bonus item, a comment-for-the-full-list keyword.
