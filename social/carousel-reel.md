# Carousel Reel · swipeable slides, animated

> **Job:** teach professionally (LinkedIn, Instagram carousels as video). **Voice:** optional. **Loops:** no. **Length:** 20–50 s.

## 1. Essence
A carousel that moves itself: each slide is a poster with one idea, and the transition between slides is a deliberate "swipe". Reads equally well as a video and as exported stills (each slide is also a PNG for a native carousel). Best on `portrait` (4:5) and `square`.

## 2. Look
- A consistent slide master: top label, headline (≤ 8 words), one visual, footer with slide index.
- A restrained palette with generous whitespace and one accent; modern sans type (OFL).
- Slide 1 is a cover that doubles as the hook; the last slide is the CTA.
- Export every slide as `slide-01.png`... in `slides/` alongside the video.

## 3. Timing grammar
- 3.5–5 s per slide; headline appears first, supporting visual 0.5 s later.
- Swipe transition 0.35 s with a tactile sound and slight parallax between layers.
- 5–8 slides total. One idea per slide; the last slide asks for one action.

## 4. Sound
Soft paper-swipe per slide, a gentle pad, a tick for the headline. Voice optional; if used, it reads the headline only.

## 5. Native moves
- **Parallax swipe.** Foreground slides faster than background during the transition. *Fits:* frameworks; steps; stories.
- **Progressive highlight.** One line of a list lights up per beat. *Fits:* checklists; principles; lessons learned.
- **Pull-quote slide.** A single sentence at poster scale with a hold. *Fits:* the thesis; the surprising claim.

## 6. Pitfalls
Slides with paragraphs; unreadable small type; inconsistent masters; claiming credentials, results or logos the user doesn't have.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *claim → 4 proofs → CTA*; *problem → framework → example*; *myth → reality × 3*. Endings: save/share prompt, a question, the cover slide again.
