# Meme Cutaway · the setup, the beat, the reaction

> **Job:** entertain, relatable humour, brand personality. **Voice:** optional. **Loops:** optional. **Length:** 8–25 s.

## 1. Essence
Comic timing with drawn stand-ins: a deadpan setup, a freeze or hard cut, an exaggerated reaction on a cutaway, a caption punchline. Original characters and original jokes only. Not a template re-skin of an existing meme, character or format owned by someone else.

## 2. Look
- Bold flat characters with simple rigs (blink, mouth shapes, a squash-and-stretch pop), drawn in code; original designs only.
- High-contrast backgrounds, thick outlines, impact-style or rounded sans caption with a heavy stroke.
- Reaction cutaways at 120–140% zoom, snapped (no easing) on the beat.

## 3. Timing grammar
- Setup ≤ 4 s, with the premise readable immediately.
- The beat: a 0.4–0.8 s freeze or silence before the cut. The pause is the joke.
- Reaction 1.5–3 s, then a caption punchline in ≤ 6 words.
- Tag/alt-ending: a 1 s button after the punchline for rewatch value.

## 4. Sound
Original stingers and foley only (record-scratch style, boing, thud, laugh-track-free). Silence before the beat; the hit lands in the exact frame of the cut.

## 5. Native moves
- **Freeze-and-zoom.** Hard stop, push-in 120%, caption. *Fits:* workplace awkwardness; "me when..."; unexpected news.
- **Split-brain.** The screen divides into what they said vs. what they meant. *Fits:* meetings; emails; customer requests.
- **Escalation ladder.** The same gag repeated, each time bigger. *Fits:* small annoyances; procrastination; scope creep.

## 6. Pitfalls
Borrowing an existing meme image, character, catchphrase or audio; jokes that punch at a real person or group; explaining the joke; a setup that runs long.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *setup-beat-reaction*; *escalation ×3*; *expectation vs. reality*. Endings: the tag, a freeze, a deadpan stare to camera.
