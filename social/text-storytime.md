# Text Storytime · chat, notes or terminal as the stage

> **Job:** entertain, hot takes, confessions, "what happened when...". **Voice:** optional. **Loops:** optional. **Length:** 20–50 s.

## 1. Essence
The story arrives as text in an interface the viewer already knows: a fictional messaging app, a notes page, a search bar, a terminal. Beats are messages. Suspense is typing indicators and pauses. Not a screenshot of a real app or a real person's messages.

## 2. Look
- Invent a generic interface: neutral bubble chat, paper-note page, or monospaced terminal. No real app brand, logo or recognisable trade dress.
- Fictional names and avatars only. Never real people, real handles or real brands.
- Messages 44–60 px, generous padding; the newest message always inside the safe area; older messages scroll up and dim.

## 3. Timing grammar
- Hook: the last message of the story, or its most absurd line, is shown first ("Then he said: ..."), then rewinds.
- Typing indicator 0.4–0.8 s before each message; message appears on a soft pop.
- Reading rule: a message stays until its characters ÷ 15 + 0.6 s have passed (readcheck).
- Pause (1–1.5 s of nothing but ambience) before the turn.

## 4. Sound
Soft pop per message (two pitches for the two sides), keyboard ticks for typing, a low pad underneath; a single stinger on the turn. Optional TTS reads the other side only.

## 5. Native moves
- **Delete and retype.** A message is erased and replaced, showing a change of mind. *Fits:* resignations; apologies; confessions.
- **Read receipt beat.** "Seen" appears and nothing follows. *Fits:* ghosting; awkward silence; waiting on news.
- **Zoom to the word.** The camera snaps to one word in a bubble. *Fits:* the punchline; the number; the betrayal.

## 6. Pitfalls
Using a real app's look; real names or screenshots of real conversations; walls of text; a story with no turn. Stories presented as true must be true or labelled as fiction.

## 7. Variation space
Structures: *rewind from the ending*; *two-person escalation*; *notes page that gets crossed out*. Endings: the final line, a question to comment on, a loop back to the first message.
